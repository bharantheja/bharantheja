About RideCompare

Why we built this

Bengaluru has 6+ major ride-hailing apps — and every commuter wastes time jumping between them to find the cheapest fare. RideCompare solves this in seconds.

All fare data is sourced from official 2025 Bengaluru rate cards. No login, no ads, no hidden charges — just instant price comparison.
